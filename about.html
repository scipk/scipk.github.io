Parham Khodadi

I am pursuing my M.S. in Aerospace Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, currently researching robust control for spacecraft rendezvous in the GLACIER Lab under Professor Shahriar Talebi. I recently graduated with my B.S. in Aerospace Engineering (Physics minor) from San Diego State University.

My work runs from linearized control design through hardware test: robust rendezvous control at UCLA, an autonomous baseball-avoidance multirotor for the NASA Langley BAM challenge as project lead, QR-fiducial navigation for drones in Python and OpenCV in the SPACE Lab, and a Solar Gravitational Lens mission design where I led flight dynamics. I am open to internship opportunities, research collaborations, and exciting aerospace projects.
