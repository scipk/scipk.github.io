About the channel

I love sharing my passion for STEM with others. On SciPK I make videos that explore STEM projects, concepts, history, and people, with the goal of making STEM loved by the masses through enjoyable, yet educational content. Two kinds of thing show up: projects, where I make something cool and discuss it, and video essays, where I explain a subject — a history, a person, an object, or a phenomenon.

My cousin used to make Persian comedy skits when I was a kid, and he would put me in his videos — that is where I learned I enjoy videography. My love for public speaking comes from doing theater in high school; granted, it was for the easy A, but I found I enjoyed being on stage. Combining those two with engineering is how SciPK started.
