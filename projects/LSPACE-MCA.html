Overview

The NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy (MCA) is an intensive five-month program that challenges participants to design and develop a complete mission concept for Mars exploration. From May to September 2022, I served as the Thermal Systems Engineer on a 12-member interdisciplinary team tasked with designing a Mars rover mission targeting lava tube exploration.

My primary focus was on the thermal management and protection of rover systems and scientific payloads operating in the harsh Martian environment. This involved calculating heat transfer across complex rover subsystems, selecting appropriate materials for passive thermal control, and designing specialized thermal dissipation hardware to meet mission constraints.

Beyond the technical work, the program provided extensive training in critical aerospace engineering disciplines including risk management, project management, and systems engineering—all essential for developing mission-ready concepts.

Learn more about the L'SPACE program at L'SPACE.
