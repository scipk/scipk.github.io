Experimental Aerodynamics

San Diego State University, Spring 2025. Taught by Dr. Xiaofeng Liu.

Overview

This two-unit course focuses on the fundamentals of experimental aerodynamics, including wind tunnel testing, data acquisition, and analysis techniques. The project involved designing and conducting experiments to validate aerodynamic theories and principles learned in other classes.

This class used MATLAB for data analysis and visualization and LaTeX for report writing.

Lab reports

Throughout the semester, I completed seven comprehensive lab reports covering various experimental aerodynamics topics. Each report includes detailed analysis, MATLAB code, and experimental results.
